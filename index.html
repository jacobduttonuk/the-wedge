Questions

Couldn't I just do this with ChatGPT?

You could write the emails with it. The hard part is knowing which five brands to write to this week: spotting the marketing director appointed on Tuesday and the funding round announced on Thursday, checking both, matching them to the right case study, and doing it all again next Monday. That's about a day of a new-business person's week. We do it for £299 a month, and you keep the judgement.

Isn't the one-pager spec work?

No. It's a short point of view that shows you've done your homework, so the first meeting is about their problem rather than your credentials deck. We draft it, so none of your team's time goes into it, and your best ideas stay for when they're paying.

Do you ever send emails for me?

Never. We don't have access to your inbox. You read everything and send it yourself, so your name and your relationships stay in your hands.

How do you pick the brands?

We track the moments a brand is most likely to bring in a new agency: a change in marketing leadership, fresh funding, a launch or market entry, a run of marketing hires, a review or a shift in campaign activity. Each signal is scored on timing and on fit with your work, and only the strongest five reach you.

Is this OK under UK GDPR and PECR?

It's built for UK B2B rules. We research companies and professional roles from public sources only. Every email we draft for you includes your identity and an opt-out. We flag sole traders and partnerships, which PECR treats differently. And because you send from your own account, you stay in control of who hears from you.
