Privacy notice

Last updated 20 November 2026

In short: we use public, professional information to help agencies get in touch with the right people at the right time. We never use home addresses, personal phone numbers or sensitive information, and you can ask us to stop at any time by emailing hello@thewedgebrief.com.

Who we are

The Wedge is a trading name of Jacob Dutton Innovation Consultancy, a sole establishment licensed in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates (trade licence CN-6732426, Abu Dhabi Registration Authority). We're the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us at hello@thewedgebrief.com.

Whose data we use, and why

Agencies that sign up. Your name, work email, agency website and anything you tell us about your work, so we can build and send your weekly packs and manage your subscription. Our lawful basis is the contract with you. If you pay, our payment provider processes your payment details; we don't see or store card numbers.

Agency owners and leaders we contact about The Wedge. Business contact details and job information from public sources, such as agency websites and professional profiles. We use them to tell you about The Wedge. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests. Every email we send has an opt-out, and we honour it straight away.

People featured in our packs. When we tell an agency that a brand may be looking for help, we include information about the relevant decision-maker at that brand: their name, job title, employer, professional background and things they've said publicly about their work, such as in interviews, trade press or company announcements. We do this so agencies can make relevant, well-timed business approaches instead of untargeted ones. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests, and we've weighed those interests against yours. We only use information about people's professional roles. We don't use home addresses, personal contact details, or information about health, beliefs or private life. Anything we post goes to a business address.

Where the information comes from

Public sources only: company websites and announcements, trade and business press, published interviews and podcasts, public professional profiles, company registries, funding news and job adverts. Every fact in a pack carries a link to its source.

Who we share it with

  • Agencies subscribed to The Wedge, who receive the packs.
  • Service providers who help us run the business: email and file hosting, website hosting, AI tools that help us research and draft, a printing and postal service, and our payment provider. They act on our instructions.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

We never sell personal data.

International transfers

We're based in the UAE and some of our providers are in the UK, the EU and the US. Where data moves between countries, we use recognised safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Subscriber details: while you subscribe, then up to 12 months, and longer for financial records where the law requires it.
  • Agency contacts we've emailed: up to 24 months from our last contact, unless you opt out sooner, in which case we keep only what we need to make sure we don't contact you again.
  • Information about people featured in packs: up to 12 months after it last appeared in a pack.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or move it to someone else. You have an absolute right to object to direct marketing, and you can object to any use based on legitimate interests. If you're featured in a pack and want us to stop, email us and we'll remove you from future packs. To use any of these rights, email hello@thewedgebrief.com. We'll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, email hello@thewedgebrief.com with the subject "Complaint". We'll acknowledge it within 30 days, look into it and tell you what we've done. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Cookies

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

If we change this notice, we'll update the date at the top.
